Highlights replies that repeat the same wording across many accounts.

What this tool does and does not tell you. Chorus reports observable facts: identical or near-identical wording posted by multiple accounts, shared sentence templates, and reply bursts. It does not determine whether an account is automated, and it deliberately contains no stylometric “was this written by AI” scoring, because such detectors are unreliable on short text and misfire on people writing in a second language. Repeated wording has innocent explanations. Read the evidence before concluding anything about a person.
